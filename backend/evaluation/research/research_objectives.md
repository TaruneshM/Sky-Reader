# 🎯 Research Objectives Specification

**Project Title:** Sky Reader: An Evidence-Grounded Agentic AI Framework for Reliable Weather Intelligence and Risk Assessment  
**Author:** Sky Reader Research Team  
**Status:** Methodological Specification  

---

## Central Research Question
> **Can an evidence-grounded agentic weather intelligence system reduce hallucinations and improve weather-risk assessment compared with a standalone LLM and a conventional tool-using agent?**

---

## Primary Research Objectives (RO)

The research objectives define what this scientific investigation aims to achieve, establish, and measure. In accordance with academic methodology, research objectives are distinct from empirical results observed during experimentation.

### RO1: Natural-Language Weather Intelligence Access
- **Goal:** Design and construct an interactive, conversational natural-language interface capable of parsing multi-faceted meteorological queries (current observations, hourly forecasts, multi-day trends, extreme comparisons, travel recommendations).
- **Scope:** Provide robust conversational handling without requiring users to construct formal API queries, select coordinates, or interpret raw meteorological JSON arrays.
- **Evaluation Mechanism:** Measured via Response Completeness Score and Qualitative Multi-Hop Interaction evaluation.

### RO2: Evidence-Grounded Generation and Formal Verification
- **Goal:** Develop an architectural mechanism that automatically captures raw telemetry returned by weather tools, compiles it into immutable, structured evidence items with unique deterministic IDs, and constructs a verifiable explanation proof linking text claims to evidence IDs.
- **Scope:** Provide an end-to-end verifiable audit trail for every meteorological statement generated by the system, ensuring transparent verification by downstream users or auditing algorithms.
- **Evaluation Mechanism:** Measured via Evidence Availability Rate, Evidence Usage Rate, and Valid Explanation Proof Rate.

### RO3: Mitigation of Meteorological Hallucinations and Contradictions
- **Goal:** Architect a closed-loop claim validation and recovery subsystem that intercepts candidate text, extracts atomic quantitative and factual propositions, audits them deterministically against retrieved evidence, and triggers automated correction or fallback.
- **Scope:** Quantitatively demonstrate whether an active validation guardrail reduces both hard contradictions (asserting values that conflict with observations) and unsupported assertions (fabricating conditions not present in retrieved telemetry).
- **Evaluation Mechanism:** Measured via Hallucination Rate, Contradiction Rate, Unsupported Claim Rate, and Clean Case Ratio (proportion of answers with zero hallucinations).

### RO4: Deterministic Weather Risk & Hazard Assessment
- **Goal:** Formulate and implement a deterministic Python-based rule engine that evaluates meteorological thresholds (extreme heat, freezing temperatures, precipitation accumulation, gale-force wind gusts) to classify hazard severity into standardized tiers (LOW, MODERATE, HIGH, SEVERE) without relying on probabilistic LLM guesswork.
- **Scope:** Eliminate dangerous severity underreporting (e.g., classifying a severe convective storm as safe) and ensure that safety precautions and official disclaimer guidelines (distinguishing AI risk assessments from government alerts) are strictly enforced.
- **Evaluation Mechanism:** Measured via Risk Classification Accuracy, Hazard Precision, Hazard Recall, and Dangerous Severity Underreporting Count across benchmark hazard cases.

### RO5: Comparative Architectural Ablation
- **Goal:** Conduct a rigorous, reproducible comparative benchmark comparing three cleanly isolated system architectures under identical model weights, inference parameters, and ground-truth atmospheric conditions:
  1. *Baseline LLM:* Zero tools, relying purely on parametric memory and epistemic boundary instructions.
  2. *Tool-Using Agent:* Conventional ReAct-style agent with retrieval tools but without evidence compilation, claim validation, or risk engines.
  3. *Complete Sky Reader:* The full pipeline incorporating deterministic meteorological analysis, evidence compilation, claim validation, hallucination control, and risk assessment.
- **Scope:** Isolate the specific performance contributions of tool retrieval versus post-retrieval verification guardrails.
- **Evaluation Mechanism:** Matched-pair case evaluation across 50 benchmark cases and 10 categories using non-parametric paired permutation tests and bootstrap confidence intervals.

### RO6: Characterization of System Trade-Offs (Accuracy, Completeness, Latency)
- **Goal:** Quantify and analyze the trade-offs inherent in multi-stage agentic verification:
  - The trade-off between aggressive numerical variable recall and conservative, grounded narrative safety fallbacks.
  - The trade-off between answer completeness and strict adherence to epistemic boundaries (refusal on impossible/fictional queries).
  - The computational latency and execution reliability penalty introduced by multi-step tool iterations and claim verification loops.
- **Scope:** Provide a transparent, scientifically honest accounting of the operational costs and practical boundaries of trustworthy agentic AI systems.
- **Evaluation Mechanism:** Evaluated via Numerical Accuracy vs. MAE, Completeness Score vs. Hallucination Rate, and Mean/P95 Latency Profiles.
